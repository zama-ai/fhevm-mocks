short: A definition that lives in the `entries/` folder of a definition folder and appears in its index.
---

A registered definition lives in a definition folder, as `entries/<ID>.md`, and appears in the index of that folder, its `README.md`. [The `reg-definitions` skill](../../../../.claude/skills/reg-definitions/SKILL.md) gives its format, and it passes `generate-index.mjs --check`.

A registered definition has an ID that never changes, aliases that the script checks, and one place where every reader finds it. It wins over every loose definition of the same term. [R-06](../../rules/entries/R-06.md) gives the rule.
