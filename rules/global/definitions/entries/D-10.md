---

A rule tag sits in the `tag` field of the front matter of each rule. It says whether the rule text is accurate, whether the code follows it, and whether a check enforces it.

[`TAGS.json`](../../../../.claude/skills/reg-rules/TAGS.json), in the folder of the `reg-rules` skill, lists the rule tags and their meanings. It is the only list.

A rule tag is not a definition tag. A definition tag says whether a definition can change or is retired. Write "definition tag" or "rule tag" wherever "tag" alone could mean either.
