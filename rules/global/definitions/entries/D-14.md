name: Canonical chain

short: The one host chain that an FHEVM group designates among its host chains, so that each group has exactly one.
---

Each FHEVM group names one of its host chains as its canonical chain. A group has exactly one, so there are as many canonical chains as groups, counted per group:

| Group     | Canonical chain             |
| --------- | --------------------------- |
| `mainnet` | Ethereum Mainnet (1)        |
| `testnet` | Ethereum Sepolia (11155111) |
| `devnet`  | Ethereum Sepolia (11155111) |

Being canonical belongs to the pair of a group and a host chain, and not to the chain alone. Ethereum Sepolia is the canonical chain of `testnet` and also of `devnet`: two groups share one chain, and each names it canonical. Every other host chain of a group is a non-canonical chain ([D-15](D-15.md)).

The canonical chain holds the group's `KMSGeneration` and the group's `ProtocolConfig`:

- `KMSGeneration`: a group has one, on its canonical chain. No other host chain of the group has one.
- `ProtocolConfig`: the canonical chain holds the group's `ProtocolConfig`. Each non-canonical chain also has a `ProtocolConfig` of its own today.

Every canonical chain is an Ethereum chain today. That is a fact about the groups as they are, not a requirement.

This definition describes the protocol as it works now. A group can name another canonical chain, and a new group names its own.
