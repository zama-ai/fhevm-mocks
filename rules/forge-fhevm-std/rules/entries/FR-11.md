title: Say what is not true
short: A declaration says when its helper ignores an argument, cannot enforce something, or holds only under a condition.
old: '2.7'
check: impossible
---

If a helper ignores an argument, cannot enforce something, or holds
only under a condition, the declaration says so. Silent divergence is the failure mode this codebase
keeps hitting.
