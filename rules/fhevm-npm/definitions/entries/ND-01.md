A generation family is a set of dev packages, one for each generation, whose payloads all publish the same npm name. Each generation lives in its own directory below the directory of the family, named after the generation, as `host-contracts-cleartext/<vN>`. [D-02](../../../global/definitions/entries/D-02.md) defines a generation, and [ND-02](ND-02.md) says which generations of a family are live.

## Examples

- ✅ `host-contracts-cleartext` is a generation family. `host-contracts-cleartext/<vN-1>` and `host-contracts-cleartext/<vN>` are two dev packages, and both payloads publish `@fhevm/host-contracts-cleartext`.
- ❌ `hardhat/v2/plugin` and `hardhat/v3/plugin` are not a generation family. Their directories follow the major versions of Hardhat, not the generations of the protocol, and their payloads publish two different names, `@fhevm/hardhat-plugin` and `@fhevm/hardhat-plugin-v3`.
- ❌ `common` is not a generation family. It is one shared helper, in one directory, with no generation in its path.

## What can change

This definition follows the way the protocol rotates its generations. If the protocol design changes how generations are numbered or kept side by side, what makes a family can change too.
