When generations share a name, only the active generation's payload sets `member: true`; the validator groups
published entries by the `name` read from their real `package.json` and rejects a group with two member entries.
Which generation is active is inventory data.

```text
✅ Two generations share one name, and only the active one is a member:
   host-contracts-cleartext/<vN>/pkg     @fhevm/host-contracts-cleartext   member: true
   host-contracts-cleartext/<vN-1>/pkg   @fhevm/host-contracts-cleartext   member: false

❌ Two members share one name:
   host-contracts-cleartext/<vN>/pkg     @fhevm/host-contracts-cleartext   member: true
   host-contracts-cleartext/<vN-1>/pkg   @fhevm/host-contracts-cleartext   member: true
```
