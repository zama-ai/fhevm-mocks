An attempt that sent transactions and was not finished goes under
`aborted/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<kind>-v<release>-<n>/`, dated by its first transaction. It never carries a `verify-report.json`
and is never a `previous`.

> **Implemented.** `fhevm-npm check deployments` checks this rule. It runs in `make check-pre`, and it is generation-free.
