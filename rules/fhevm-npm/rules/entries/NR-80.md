The manifest's `deploymentId` is the stack, its `chainId` the chain folder's, the config's `admin`
the sealed one. In an event folder, the manifest's `version` is the generation, `<major>.<minor>` (`0.14`, `1.0`), and
its `packageVersion` `<major>.<minor>.<p>`, as the folder name says: `v14.1` is `0.14.1`, `v1.0.0` is `1.0.0`. For now,
a deploy's manifest may omit `packageVersion`, and then no manifest field checks the patch in its folder name; an
upgrade or hotfix event's may not.

> **Implemented.** `fhevm-npm check deployments` checks this rule. It runs in `make check-pre`, and it is generation-free.
