An upgrade moves exactly one generation up; a hotfix stays in its generation with
a higher patch. Over the sequence, `(generation, patch)` strictly increases and dates never go back. Between consecutive
events that both have an `event.json`, defining blocks strictly increase (for now, a deploy may lack one: [NR-79](NR-79.md)).

> **Implemented.** `fhevm-npm check deployments` checks this rule. It runs in `make check-pre`, and it is generation-free.
