`deployments/<chain>/<stack>/`, where `<chain>` is a host chain of
`fhevm-chains.config.json` and `<stack>` the stack's `deploymentId`. Under a stack: event folders, `aborted/` and a
`README.md`, nothing else.

> **Implemented.** `fhevm-npm check deployments` checks this rule. It runs in `make check-pre`, and it is generation-free.
