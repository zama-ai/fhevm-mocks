`<kind>.config.json` with `deploymentId` = the stack and a relative `outDir`
inside the folder; `tool-commit.txt` whose first line is a full commit (equal to the manifest's `toolCommit` when it has
one); the out dir holds `manifest.json`, `addresses.sol`, `journal.jsonl` and `event.json`, and for an upgrade or hotfix
event `progress.jsonl` and `verify-report.json`; no forge scratch, no `.git`. For now, a deploy may omit `event.json`;
an upgrade, a hotfix and an attempt of any kind may not.

> **Implemented.** `fhevm-npm check deployments` checks this rule. It runs in `make check-pre`, and it is generation-free.
