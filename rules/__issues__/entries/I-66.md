## Gap

A deploy without `event.json` has no `defining` block for the check, so the block-order check between it and the next event is skipped (today: the v13 deploy and the v14 upgrade). A deploy's `defining` is its `last` transaction (8.2.3), and the journal records every transaction's block, so the check can read it there.

## Where

`fhevm-npm/base/checks/deployments.ts:291-296`, `:605-609`

## Fix

When a deploy has no `event.json`, take its `defining` block from the highest `block` of its journal's transactions. Only the folder-date check, which needs chain time, still skips it.

Affects [NR-82](../../fhevm-npm/rules/entries/NR-82.md).
