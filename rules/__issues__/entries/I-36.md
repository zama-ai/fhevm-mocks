Does a rule that repeats one sentence of another rule count as `CONFLICT`?

- **Yes:** FR-04, FR-07, FR-08, FR-13, FR-26, FR-28, and FR-34 become `CONFLICT`.
- **No:** they keep their tags. The tagging leaves this decision out of FR-04, FR-07, FR-13, FR-26, and FR-34, although it names them.

The reviews disagree on the current tags of some of these rules. The tag in each rule is a vote, not a verdict, until this decision.

Affects [FR-04](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-04.md), [FR-07](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-07.md), [FR-08](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-08.md), [FR-13](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-13.md), [FR-26](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-26.md), [FR-28](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-28.md), [FR-34](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-34.md).
