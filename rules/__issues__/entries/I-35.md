Is an accurate history passage, such as the removed event replay, a retired passage or acceptable rationale?

- **Retired:** FR-07, FR-13, and FR-39 `STALE`.
- **Rationale:** they keep their other tags, and FR-39 `HOLD`.

The reviews disagree on the current tags of some of these rules. The tag in each rule is a vote, not a verdict, until this decision.

Affects [FR-07](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-07.md), [FR-13](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-13.md), [FR-39](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-39.md).
