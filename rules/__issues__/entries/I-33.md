Are the 25 `bytes32` batch overloads of `decrypt` and `decryptPublic` the exception "the type cannot be known"?

- **Yes:** FR-12 `STALE`, because their names must say so, and FR-14 `HOLD`.
- **No:** both `VIOLATED`.

The reviews disagree on the current tags of some of these rules. The tag in each rule is a vote, not a verdict, until this decision.

Affects [FR-12](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-12.md), [FR-14](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-14.md).
