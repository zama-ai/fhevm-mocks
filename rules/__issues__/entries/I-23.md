title: A tag for rules not reviewed yet
short: Every rule must carry a real tag, even while it waits for a review or a decision from the owner.
old: K5
---

Decide whether a rule tag for "not reviewed yet" or "waiting on the owner" is needed. The issues registry may cover that need: a rule waiting on a decision lists the issue in its `issues` field.
