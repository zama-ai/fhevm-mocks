Does "carries `Forge` in its name" apply to every file in `pkg/src` that uses the VM, or only to host source and `_host/`?

- **Every file:** FR-05 and FR-29 `VIOLATED`.
- **Host source and `_host/` only:** FR-05 `STALE`, and FR-29 `CONFLICT` until its duplicate is removed.

Affects [FR-05](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-05.md), [FR-29](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-29.md).
