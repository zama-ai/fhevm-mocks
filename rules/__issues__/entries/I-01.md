title: Which roots get a definition
short: The terms "workspace root" and "installation root" have no definition, and the old one of "workspace root" is wrong.
old: Q1
---

The manifest lists three workspace roots: `.`, `./hardhat/v2`, and `./hardhat/v3`. NR-69 speaks of a nested workspace root. "Installation root" appears in 14 rules and has no definition. The old npm book defined "workspace root" as "`sdk/` itself", which is wrong.

- **Answer A (recommended):** "Workspace root" and "Installation root" become global definitions, because R-02 and FR-28 use the workspace root too.
- **Answer B:** both become npm definitions, after the package kinds.
