Is the bytecode patching meant for all three upgrade paths, or only for the one-generation upgrade?

- **All three:** FR-26 `STALE`.
- **One only:** FR-26 `VIOLATED`.

Affects [FR-26](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-26.md).
