## Gap

The rules date an upgrade by its `ACLOwner.upgrade` transaction, but the check never verifies that `defining` is that transaction; and the folder day comes from `event.json`'s `blockTimestamp`, which the check trusts (it runs offline). Predates D8.

## Where

`fhevm-npm/base/checks/deployments.ts:346-357`

## Fix

Check that an upgrade's or hotfix's `defining` is the journal's `ACLOwner.upgrade` transaction; say in 8.2.3 that the block timestamp is the tool's chain read.

Affects [NR-77](../../fhevm-npm/rules/entries/NR-77.md), [NR-81](../../fhevm-npm/rules/entries/NR-81.md).
