Does the gas-metering rule bind the cleartext mock contracts and the `view` helpers?

- **Mocks bound:** FR-07 `VIOLATED`, because the mocks resume metering unconditionally.
- **Exempt:** FR-07 and FR-34 `CONFLICT` until the exemption is written.

Affects [FR-07](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-07.md), [FR-34](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-34.md).
