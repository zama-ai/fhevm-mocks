title: "Whether `checked-by` is required with `check: script`"
short: "A rule can claim `check: script` without naming its check, because `checked-by` is optional."
old: K1
---

An earlier review asked to require `checked-by` whenever `check: script`. Today 73 rules have `check: script` and no `checked-by`. Requiring it is a change to the `KIND.json` of the rules kind, after which the script lists every rule that breaks it.
