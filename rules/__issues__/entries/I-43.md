## Gap

The validator requires a `clean` script on every dev owner of a published payload, mirror owners included. Rule 5.1.4c exempts mirror owners, so for them the check fails with no rule behind it.

## Where

`fhevm-npm/base/checks/scripts.ts:56`

## Fix

Exempt mirror owners in the check, or remove the exemption from the rule.

Affects [NR-43c](../../fhevm-npm/rules/entries/NR-43c.md).
