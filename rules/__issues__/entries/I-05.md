old: Q9

The old "Goal" lists six aims: no tarballs, consumer fidelity, repository hygiene, no version drift, automatic validation, and CI-ready.

- **Decide, line by line:** which lines are binding. Each binding line becomes a rule in the group it fits. Each other line goes into `fhevm-npm/WORKSPACE.md` as an aim.
- **Decide:** whether "never commit a `.tgz` file" becomes a rule. Recommended: yes, in the Packages group.
