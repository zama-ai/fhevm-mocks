title: Whether a rule can be split into sub-rules
short: A split into sub-rules narrows what the parent ID means, against the principle that an ID never changes meaning.
old: K3
---

A rule with parts of different status is split into sub-rules, as `FR-19a`, and tags per passage are dropped. The books already hold ten sub-rules.

- **Allowed:** the sub-rules stay, and the skill says that a split narrows the parent.
- **Not allowed:** each split parent is retired, and new IDs replace the parent and its sub-rules.
