Does "custom errors over `require` strings" cover the argument checks in the public mixins?

- **Yes:** FR-32 `VIOLATED`.
- **No:** FR-32 `STALE`, or `CONFLICT` with FR-15. The tagging also leaves out FR-15, which this decision names.

Affects [FR-32](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-32.md), [FR-15](../../forge-fhevm-std/rules/entries/FR-15.md).
